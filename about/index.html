    Hi, I’m Maria#

    I am a London-based Coach, Trainer, and Mindfulness Teacher dedicated to helping individuals and teams navigate growth with clarity and presence. My work bridges the gap between professional excellence and personal wellbeing.

    Currently, I focus on three core pillars:

    • Professional Coaching Education As a Senior Faculty member at Coaching Outside the Box, I train and mentor coaches toward their ICF credentials.
    • Wellness & Recovery I serve as a Recovery Coach, Trainer, and Mindfulness Teacher at Reframe.
    • Movement & Stillness I lead yoga and mindfulness sessions within local London studios and corporate offices.
